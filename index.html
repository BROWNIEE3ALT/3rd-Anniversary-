Chapter one · before we knew

The girl on the way to school

✦

Some stories begin with a hello. Some begin with a message. But theirs?

Theirs began with a boy who didn't even know a girl's name.

He was in 10th class when he first saw her. She went to a different school, but her way passed by his school. And that day, he found himself wondering, “Who is she?”

People say love happens at first sight. Maybe he wouldn't have believed it back then… but something about that girl made him curious.

And then, something changed.

The boy who was always late to school, the one who got punished for it, suddenly started going to school ONE WHOLE HOUR EARLY.

Why? Just to see her. 🥹❤️

Every morning, he would arrive early, hoping to catch a glimpse of a girl he hadn't even spoken to yet. And she had no idea that somewhere along her way to school, someone was already making her a reason to wake up early.

Maybe their story had already started, even before either of them knew it.

The boy who suddenly learned how to be early. 🤭
Chapter two · getting closer

Strangers, then home

♡

After some months, he finally got her contact. They started talking, and slowly, those conversations became a part of their everyday lives.

They shared their days, their problems, their little happiness, and the things they couldn't always tell everyone else.

In just a few days, or maybe a few weeks, they grew so close.

Maybe she was getting attached to him, too. Maybe neither of them understood just how special it was becoming.

But there was something he felt from the very beginning.

Even when they were strangers, she felt like a part of him. She felt familiar, safe… like home.

It was almost as if he had known her long before they ever met.

Soulmates, maybe? Haha. Who knows? 🤭❤️

But sometimes, the heart recognizes something before the mind can explain it.

And then came the day that changed everything.

The night everything changed

☾

And then, the moment he had been dreaming of finally arrived.

Not a grand stage. Not fireworks. Just a date, a time, a dark sky—and a new beginning.

And just like that, his dream came true.

SHE CAME INTO HIS LIFE. ❤️

He would never forget that moment. The happiness he felt was like that of a little kid who had just received the most precious gift in the world.

And maybe, without even realizing it, two people who once knew nothing about each other had just begun writing a story that would mean everything to them.

A little hug for the beginning of forever. 🧸
Chapter three · all the pages since

Look at us now

✦

And then, she became his life, and he became hers.

They loved each other in a way that felt so big, so beautiful, and so difficult to explain. There were sweet moments, endless longing, tears, and times when things weren't easy.

They cried. They held on. Sometimes things hurt, sometimes they had to find their way back to each other.

But somehow, through it all, they kept choosing to stay.

And look at them now.

Who would've thought that one girl walking past a school would become such a huge part of one boy's world?

Who would've thought that a boy who used to be late every day would one day remember a date, a time, and even the moon from the night his life changed?

Funny how life works, isn't it? 🍂🌷
